Privacy Policy for Trusí appka

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trusí appka ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Application. It also explains your choices and privacy rights.

Trusí appka is a team application for managing football-team activities, statistics, attendance, challenges, achievements, notifications, and related team content. Some features are optional and require separate permission or consent.

Health and fitness data (Health Data)

Step challenge and Health Connect

The Application offers an optional team step-challenge feature. If you choose to enable it and grant the requested system permissions, the Application accesses only your step-count data through Health Connect on Android or Apple Health/HealthKit on Apple devices. The Application does not read routes, precise location, or any other Health Connect or HealthKit data for this feature.

When you first enable the feature, the Application requests access to historical Health Data and reads daily step totals for up to the previous 30 days. This historical access is used to populate your initial step history, team statistics, step-related achievements, and leaderboard instead of starting with an empty history. While the feature remains enabled, the Application updates the totals when you use it. If you separately allow background health-data access, the Application may also synchronize your latest daily totals approximately every six hours, including when the Application is closed or not actively in use. Operating system scheduling may delay or prevent a background synchronization.

The following information is transmitted to and stored on the Trusí appka server:

  • the calendar date and daily step count;
  • the source of the total (Health Connect or HealthKit);
  • your time zone and the time through which the total was measured; and
  • the status of your consent and health-data permission.

This information is linked to your Trusí appka account. We use it only to provide your step history, the team step challenge and leaderboard, team statistics, and step-related achievements. Members of the same team may see your display name, daily step totals and recent history, aggregate totals and averages, and position in the team leaderboard. They do not receive your route or other Health Connect data.

We do not sell Health Data. We do not transfer it to advertising platforms or data brokers, and we do not use it for advertising, marketing, creditworthiness, lending, employment, insurance, or medical diagnosis or treatment. Health Data is not used to build advertising profiles.

Your Health Data controls

You can stop future step synchronization at any time by turning off "Share my steps with the team" in the Application. You can also revoke the Application's permissions in Health Connect or the Apple Health settings. Turning synchronization off stops new collection but does not automatically delete step totals already stored on our server. You can request deletion of stored step data or delete your account as described in Delete your personal data.

Optional connected fitness services

If you separately connect an available third-party fitness service such as Footbar or Strava, the Application may receive the activity data you authorize that service to provide. Depending on the connected service, this can include an activity identifier, activity name and type, date and time, duration, distance, speed, calories, heart-rate measurements, session statistics, and activity location. We use connected-service data to display and compare football and fitness statistics requested by you. These integrations are separate from the Health Connect step permission and are governed by the permissions and privacy terms shown during the connection process.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use an account, we process information such as your email address, display name, internal user identifier, Firebase identifier, authentication data, team membership, team roles, and account settings. Password authentication is provided with the assistance of Firebase Authentication. We use this information to create, secure, and manage your account and to give you access to the appropriate team features.

Team and user-provided content

We process information that you or authorized team members enter into the Application. This may include team and player information, match and attendance information, football statistics, goals, fines, beverages, achievements, comments, reactions, notification preferences, and similar team-management content. This information is used to provide the features in which it was entered and may be visible to other authorized members of the same team.

TrusBot and voice input

When you use TrusBot, we store your questions and the generated answers. Your question and the team information needed to answer it may be sent to OpenAI as our artificial intelligence service provider. Do not include information in a question that you do not want processed for this purpose.

If you choose voice dictation, the Application accesses the microphone only after you tap the dictation control and grant permission. Speech recognition is performed using the speech-recognition service available on your device. Trusí appka does not intentionally store the raw audio recording; the recognized text is handled like a question that you type and is sent only after you choose to submit it. The device or speech-recognition provider may process audio under its own privacy terms.

Device, notification, and diagnostic information

We may process your operating-system version, device manufacturer and model, Application version, IP address contained in ordinary server logs, push-notification token and device identifier, notification delivery information, crash logs, stack traces, performance diagnostics, and technical events. We use this information to communicate with the Application, deliver notifications you enable, secure the service, diagnose failures, and improve reliability. Health-sync diagnostics may indicate that a synchronization was attempted or failed and for which date, but they are not intended to include the number of steps.

How we use information

We use information described in this Policy to:

  • provide and maintain the Application and its team features;
  • create, authenticate, and manage accounts and permissions;
  • provide optional step, fitness, notification, and TrusBot features;
  • display information to authorized members of the same team;
  • respond to support, access, correction, and deletion requests;
  • detect misuse, protect accounts, and comply with applicable law; and
  • diagnose errors and improve the security and reliability of the Application.

Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process account and team information as necessary to provide the service requested by you, health and fitness data on the basis of your explicit consent, security and limited diagnostic information for our legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service, and information when necessary to comply with a legal obligation. You may withdraw consent for optional processing at any time without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

How we disclose information

Other members of your team

Information submitted for collaborative team features is shared with authorized members of the same team as described in the relevant feature and in this Policy. Step information is shared only after you enable the step feature.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the Application. Depending on the feature, these may include Google Firebase for authentication, push messaging, and crash diagnostics; OpenAI for TrusBot responses; our cloud and database hosting providers; the platform's speech-recognition provider; and Footbar or Strava when you choose to connect those services. They may process only the information necessary to provide their service to us or the connection requested by you, subject to their agreements and applicable law.

Legal requirements and organizational changes

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid request from a public authority, to protect users and the service, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of the Application. Where required, we will provide notice and require the recipient to respect this Policy and applicable law.

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data. We do not share personal or sensitive user data with advertising platforms or data brokers.

Retention of information

We retain account, team, step, connected-fitness, and TrusBot information while your account is active and as needed to provide the features you use. Some team records may also need to be retained to preserve shared team statistics. When data is no longer needed, or when we complete a valid deletion request, we delete or anonymize it unless a longer period is required for security, dispute resolution, or a legal obligation. Push-notification tokens are retained until they are replaced, invalidated, or deleted with the account. Diagnostic data is retained according to the limited retention settings of the applicable diagnostic service.

Delete your personal data

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data from the account menu in the Application by choosing "Delete account". If you cannot access the Application, you can initiate an account or Health Data deletion request by emailing matej.jandak@seznam.cz. Please send the request from the email address associated with your account so that we can verify the request without collecting additional identification documents.

A completed account deletion includes the account's personal data and stored step data, subject only to information that must be retained for a legal obligation, security, fraud prevention, or the integrity of records shared with other team members. Where a shared record must remain, we will remove or anonymize its link to the deleted account where reasonably possible. Deleting data from our server does not delete the original records held by Health Connect, Apple Health, Footbar, or Strava; you must manage those records in the respective service.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of your personal data; restrict or object to certain processing; and withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the data-protection authority in your country. To exercise these rights, contact us using the address below.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal and sensitive user data. Data transmitted between the Application and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you believe that a child has provided personal data without the required authorization, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

The Application may link to services that we do not operate. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies. Review those policies before connecting an account or providing information to those services.
